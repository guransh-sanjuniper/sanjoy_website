# Sanjoy Brand Guidelines

Sanjoy is a business transformation company powered by AI.

The brand should communicate trust, enterprise scale, simplicity, confidence, business outcomes, and long-term reliability. Sanjoy should never feel like a futuristic AI startup, a sci-fi dashboard, or a collection of disconnected AI tools.

The core brand idea is:

**Business First. AI Second.**

AI is the infrastructure behind the outcome. The business result is always the message.

---

## Brand Personality

Sanjoy should feel:

- Trusted
- Clear
- Enterprise-grade
- Calm
- Confident
- Practical
- Outcome-driven
- Long-term

Sanjoy should feel like a company that understands how modern businesses operate across departments, not just how AI models work.

The brand should be premium without being decorative, modern without being futuristic, and confident without sounding exaggerated.

---

## Tone Of Voice

The voice is professional, simple, and direct.

Use language that a business leader, operator, or enterprise decision-maker can immediately understand. The writing should focus on business outcomes, organizational clarity, and measurable value.

Preferred tone:

- Clear over clever
- Confident over excited
- Practical over abstract
- Strategic over technical
- Outcome-led over feature-led

Avoid hype, buzzwords, and vague claims. Do not make AI sound magical. Sanjoy should speak like a reliable enterprise partner.

Good messaging examples:

- "AI infrastructure for business transformation."
- "Deploy AI across every department with clarity and control."
- "Improve productivity, reduce costs, and unlock new revenue."
- "A business operating layer powered by AI."

Avoid messaging like:

- "The future of AI is here."
- "Supercharge everything with next-gen intelligence."
- "Revolutionary AI agents for the new era."
- "Unlock limitless AI power."

---

## Design Philosophy

The design system should make Sanjoy feel like a premium enterprise business platform.

The design should be inspired by Apple, OpenAI, Stripe, Anthropic, and Linear, but should not copy any one of them directly.

Design priorities:

- Whitespace does most of the work.
- Typography carries the message.
- Color is used sparingly.
- Surfaces are clean and trustworthy.
- Motion is calm and purposeful.
- Components feel durable and reusable.
- Visual hierarchy is obvious without being loud.

The interface should feel quiet, organized, and confident. Every section should help the visitor understand how Sanjoy can transform their business with AI.

---

## Color Usage

Sanjoy's color system is built around neutral enterprise foundations with a restrained coral brand accent.

Core colors:

- Primary brand: `#E84D4D`
- Brand hover: `#D93F3F`
- Light brand surface: `#FDECEC`
- Primary text: `#111111`
- Secondary text: `#5C5C5C`
- Background: `#FAFAFA`
- Surface: `#FFFFFF`
- Border: `#E8E8E8`

Color rules:

- Use black and white as the primary interface colors.
- Use coral only for brand moments, secondary CTAs, small highlights, badges, and selective emphasis.
- Use the light coral surface for gentle brand emphasis, not large decorative blocks.
- Keep borders subtle and consistent.
- Avoid large areas of saturated color.
- Avoid glowing colors, neon effects, and high-saturation AI gradients.

Primary CTAs should use black backgrounds with white text. Coral is reserved for secondary CTAs and brand accents.

The overall impression should be calm and premium, not colorful or flashy.

---

## Typography Principles

Sanjoy uses Geist for both interface and editorial typography.

Typography should feel confident, editorial, and highly readable.

Principles:

- Hero headlines should be large, direct, and business-focused.
- Headings should use tight tracking and strong hierarchy.
- Body text should be readable, calm, and spacious.
- Avoid overly small text in important messaging areas.
- Avoid decorative type treatments.
- Let short, confident phrases carry the story.

Headlines should communicate outcomes and positioning. Body copy should explain value clearly without overexplaining.

Good headline qualities:

- Short
- Clear
- Confident
- Outcome-oriented
- Easy to scan

Avoid headlines that are overly technical, playful, or hype-driven.

---

## Motion Principles

Motion should be calm, subtle, and purposeful.

Use:

- Fade
- Slide
- Opacity
- Gentle scale

Motion should support clarity and flow. It should help content appear smoothly without drawing attention to itself.

Avoid:

- Bounce
- Elastic easing
- Overshoot
- Spinning
- Glowing pulses
- Playful transitions
- Complex sci-fi dashboard animation

Animations should feel premium and controlled. The user should notice the smoothness, not the animation itself.

---

## Iconography

Icons should be minimal, geometric, and functional.

Icon principles:

- Use simple line icons.
- Keep stroke weights consistent.
- Prefer business, workflow, infrastructure, department, and outcome metaphors.
- Use icons to clarify meaning, not decorate empty space.
- Keep icons small and understated.

Avoid:

- Futuristic robot icons
- Glowing AI symbols
- Complex 3D icons
- Gaming-style icons
- Overuse of sparkles, magic wands, or abstract neural graphics

Icons should support enterprise clarity.

---

## Illustration Style

Illustrations should be abstract, structured, and business-oriented.

Preferred illustration qualities:

- Minimal
- Systematic
- Clean
- Architectural
- Department or workflow oriented
- Built from simple shapes, cards, grids, and lines

Illustrations may show business systems, operating layers, connected departments, process improvement, or AI infrastructure. They should never feel like sci-fi machinery.

Avoid:

- Neon network clouds
- Blue glowing brains
- Humanoid robots
- Cyberpunk scenes
- Busy dashboards
- Overly technical model diagrams

Illustrations should communicate organization and confidence.

---

## Photography Style

Photography, if used, should feel real, premium, and business-oriented.

Preferred photography:

- Modern office environments
- Leadership teams
- Operators and knowledge workers
- Calm collaborative moments
- Enterprise settings
- Natural light
- Neutral palettes

Photography should feel human and credible, not staged or overly futuristic.

Avoid:

- Stock photos with exaggerated emotion
- Holograms and glowing interface overlays
- Robotic imagery
- Dark sci-fi command centers
- Startup cliches
- Generic "AI future" visuals

Photography should reinforce trust and business transformation.

---

## UI Principles

The UI should feel like a premium enterprise platform.

Principles:

- Use generous whitespace.
- Keep layouts simple and structured.
- Make hierarchy clear through size, spacing, and weight.
- Use subtle borders to define surfaces.
- Use shadows sparingly.
- Avoid visual clutter.
- Keep interactions predictable.
- Prioritize readability on every screen size.

The interface should feel calm and scalable. Every UI element should look like it belongs in a serious business platform.

---

## Component Philosophy

Components should be reusable, restrained, and consistent.

Component principles:

- Build from shared CSS variables and Tailwind tokens.
- Prefer semantic variants over one-off styling.
- Keep component APIs simple.
- Use large rounded corners where appropriate.
- Use subtle shadows and borders.
- Avoid heavy glassmorphism.
- Avoid decorative gradients unless they are extremely subtle and brand-aligned.

Buttons, cards, badges, and sections should feel like part of one system. Components should support enterprise trust, not visual novelty.

Primary actions should be visually clear. Secondary actions should support the flow without competing for attention.

---

## Spacing Philosophy

Spacing is central to the Sanjoy brand.

The layout should breathe. Whitespace should create confidence, clarity, and premium feel.

Spacing principles:

- Use generous section spacing.
- Keep content widths controlled.
- Avoid dense page sections.
- Give important messages room.
- Use consistent gutters across breakpoints.
- Group related content through proximity.
- Separate major ideas with meaningful whitespace.

Whitespace should make the website feel considered and enterprise-grade.

---

## What To Avoid

Avoid anything that makes Sanjoy feel like another AI startup.

Do not use:

- Neon colors
- Blue glowing technology effects
- Cyberpunk visuals
- Sci-fi dashboards
- Gaming UI patterns
- Busy particle backgrounds
- Excessive gradients
- Heavy glassmorphism
- Overuse of sparkles or magic metaphors
- Loud shadows
- Playful motion
- Hype-driven copy
- Feature lists without business context
- Dense enterprise jargon

Sanjoy should not look like it is selling AI novelty. It should look like it is helping serious businesses transform.

---

## What Success Looks Like

A successful Sanjoy website should make visitors think:

**"This is the company that can transform my business with AI."**

It should not make visitors think:

**"This is another AI startup."**

Success means:

- The brand feels trustworthy within seconds.
- The message is easy to understand.
- Business outcomes are clearer than AI features.
- The visual system feels premium but not flashy.
- The interface feels scalable across many product areas.
- Components feel consistent across the entire website.
- AI feels like the operating layer behind business transformation.

Every future page and component should reinforce this idea: Sanjoy helps modern businesses adopt AI across departments with clarity, confidence, and measurable impact.
